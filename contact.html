Contact

How to reach the iLike team.

Get in touch

Email ilike.team789@gmail.com if you are locked out, found a problem, have a question, or want an invite.

Problems with a person

Use Report or Block in the app (the ⋯ button on posts, messages and profiles). Moderators will look at it.

Delete your account

You can do this in the app, or by email. See Delete your account.

Emergencies

If someone is in danger, contact your local emergency services first.
