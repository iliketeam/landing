Questions

Is iLike free?

Yes. iLike is free to use.

Is there an iPhone app on the App Store?

Not yet. On iPhone, open iLike in Safari and add it to your Home Screen. Steps here.

Is there an Android app?

You can install iLike from Chrome today. A direct Android download (APK) is coming soon and will be posted on the install page.

How do I update iLike?

You don't have to. It updates itself when you open it with an internet connection.

Other iLike members can see what you post. Read the Privacy Policy for the details.
