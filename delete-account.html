Delete your account

You are in control of your account and your data.

In the app

  1. Open iLike and go to Settings.
  2. Choose Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted.

If you can't get into the app

Email us from the address you signed up with, using the subject Delete my account, and include your username. We may ask you to confirm it is you. We will delete your account and let you know when it is done.

Email a deletion request

What gets deleted

  • Your profile, including your photo and banner.
  • Your posts, comments and replies, photos and videos, likes and follows.

What may remain

  • Copies in backups for a short time, until they are replaced.
  • Limited records we need for safety or to follow the law, for example about reports of abuse.

Deleting is permanent. We can't bring an account back after it is deleted.

You can also delete single posts in the app without deleting your account. To ask for a copy of your data, contact us. More in our Privacy Policy.
