Find people who like what you like.

iLike is a social app for sharing posts, photos and videos, chatting with people, and finding events. It is free to use and made for Sri Lanka.

What you can do

Everything around the things you like

Share what you like

Post your thoughts with photos or video. Like, reply, and tag friends with @.

Chat with people

Send messages and join small circle chats with people who share your taste.

Find events

See what is happening, join the event chat, and share photos from the night.

Make it yours

Set your photo and banner, and keep your posts, likes and photos together on your profile.

How to join

Three steps

  1. Get an invite.
    iLike is invite-only for now. Ask a friend who is already on iLike, or request one from us.
  2. Open the app.
    Go to the web app and create your account with your invite.
  3. Install it.
    Add iLike to your Home Screen so it opens like any other app. Here is how.
