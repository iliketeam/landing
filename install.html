Install

Get iLike on your phone

iLike works in your browser and installs like an app. Pick your device.

AndroidiPhoneComputerProblems?

Android

Option 1: Install from your browser (works today)

  1. Open the iLike web app in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
  3. Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  4. Confirm. iLike now appears with your other apps.

Option 2: Download the Android app (APK)

Coming soon

We are preparing a direct download for Android. When it is ready, the download button will appear here, along with the version number. It will not be on Google Play yet, so Android will ask you to allow installs from your browser or file manager. Only install files that you downloaded from this page.

iPhone

Apple only allows this from Safari.

  1. Open the iLike web app in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add. Open iLike from your Home Screen.

Computer

In Chrome or Edge, open the web app and click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, then Install. Or just keep it as a normal browser tab.

Problems?
