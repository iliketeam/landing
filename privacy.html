Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 November 2026

This policy explains what information iLike collects, why, and what choices you have. iLike is a small, invite-only social app. We try to collect as little as we need and we do not sell your data.

1. Who we are

"iLike", "we" and "us" means the iLike team. You can reach us at YOUR-EMAIL@example.com. This policy covers the iLike web app, the iLike Android app, and this website.

2. What we collect

Account details. When you sign up we collect the details you enter, such as your email address, your username and your password. Passwords are handled by our sign-in provider and are not stored in readable form.

Profile. Your name, bio, profile photo, banner, and any other details you choose to add.

Content you create. Posts, photos, videos, comments and replies, messages and circle chats, likes, follows, and your activity in events.

Technical information. Basic information that is sent automatically when you use iLike, such as your IP address, browser or device type, and app version. Our hosting providers may keep standard server logs.

On your device. iLike stores some data on your device (for example settings, drafts and a small cache) so the app loads faster. You can clear it from your browser or device settings.

We do not run advertising trackers. If we add analytics or notifications later, we will update this policy first.

3. How we use it

  • To run iLike: create your account, show your profile and posts, and deliver messages.
  • To keep the community safe: handle reports, remove harmful content, and prevent spam and abuse.
  • To support you and to fix problems and improve iLike.
  • To meet legal obligations.

4. Who can see your information

Other members. Your profile and posts can be seen by other iLike members. Messages are meant to be seen only by the people in that chat. Think before you share anything you would not want others to see.

Moderators. iLike moderators can review content that is reported and can act on it.

Service providers. We use trusted companies to run iLike. They process data for us and only for that purpose:

  • Supabase for our database, sign-in and file storage.
  • Cloudflare to host the web app, and GitHub to host this website.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr to load fonts and code libraries. These services can see your IP address when they deliver files to your browser.

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information if the law requires it or to protect people's safety.

Our providers may process data on servers outside Sri Lanka.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. If you delete a post, it is removed from iLike. If you delete your account, we remove your profile and the content linked to it. Copies may remain in backups for a short time, and we may keep limited records where needed for safety or the law.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Edit your profile and delete your posts in the app.
  • Delete your account in the app. See Delete your account.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct it, by emailing us.

Depending on where you live, including under Sri Lanka's Personal Data Protection Act, you may have additional rights. Email us and we will help.

7. Children

iLike is not for children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email us and we will remove it.

8. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, including secure connections and access controls. No online service is completely secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

9. Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we will update the date above and tell members in the app or on this page.

10. Contact

Questions about your privacy? Email YOUR-EMAIL@example.com.
